HIGHLANDS & TREKKING

Imatong Mountains

← Back to All Destinations
Eastern Equatoria
Nov – March
Required (TDP)
Torit / Juba

Overview

The Imatong Mountains feature Mount Kinyeti, South Sudan’s highest peak standing at 3,187 meters. Covered in lush cloud forests and sub-alpine vegetation, the region offers unparalleled trekking, endemic flora, and rich cultural interactions with local Lotuko mountain communities.

Key Highlights

  • Summit Trekking to Mount Kinyeti
  • Guided forest walks with local Lotuko trackers
  • Rich birdlife, including rare highland species

Regional Ecosystem & Circuit Connectivity

This destination forms an integral part of the Eastern Equatoria overland exploration trail, connecting seamlessly with Torit cultural experiences and the broader migration corridors near Boma National Park.
