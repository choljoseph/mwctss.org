Where to Stay

Browse verified lodging options, from executive international hotels in Juba to field camps and regional guest houses across South Sudan.

Juba Capital City

Central Hub & Entry Point
Luxury Hotel

Radisson Blu Hotel Juba

Full-service executive hospitality located near key administrative centers and the White Nile waterfront. Offers full security and event infrastructure.

Location: Central Juba
Riverside Lodge

Nile River Lodge

Boutique accommodation situated right on the banks of the Bahr el Jebel (White Nile), featuring garden dining and boat access.

Location: Nile Riverfront
Business Hotel

Pyramid Continental Hotel

Modern high-rise hotel offering conference facilities, international dining, and central access for business travelers and tour departures.

Location: Downtown Juba

National Parks & Wildlife Reserves

Boma, Badingilo, Nimule & Sudd
Expedition Camp

Mobile Safari Tented Camps

Fully equipped mobile camp setups operated by licensed safari companies in Boma and Badingilo National Parks with solar power and field logistics.

Location: Boma / Badingilo Corridor
Eco Lodge

Nimule Eco Camp

Rustic riverside bungalows and campsites overlooking the White Nile, positioned for launch boat tours to Opassi Island and white rhino tracking.

Location: Nimule Border Town
River Camp

Sudd Wetlands Field Camps

Temporary waterborne or island encampments organized for specialized research, wildlife photography, and fishing expeditions in Jonglei state.

Location: Jonglei / Sudd Marshes

Eastern Equatoria & Highlands

Torit, Kapoeta & Imatong Mountains
Community Lodge

Torit Guest Houses

Basic regional lodging at the foot of Mount Kinyeti and the Imatong Range, providing staging points for mountain trekkers and botanical expeditions.

Location: Torit Town
Cultural Base Camp

Kapoeta Outpost Lodges

Modest accommodations serving visitors exploring Toposa cultural sites, local markets, and mineral regions in Eastern Equatoria.

Location: Kapoeta

Regional & Cultural Centers

Terekeka, Yambio & Wau
Mundari Field Camp

Terekeka Cattle Camp Lodging

Mobile tented accommodation coordinated with local Mundari leaders for photography tours along the Nile cattle camps.

Location: Terekeka County
Heritage Lodge

Yambio Guest Outposts

Quiet green guest houses in Western Equatoria catering to visitors exploring the historic Azande Kingdom and tropical forest belts.

Location: Yambio
