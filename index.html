About Our Organization

Ministry of Wildlife Conservation & Tourism

Dedicated to sustainable tourism, environmental preservation, and structural eco-development in South Sudan.

The Ministry of Wildlife Conservation and Tourism is committed to protecting South Sudan’s natural heritage, preserving its world-renowned ecosystems, and facilitating responsible, community-focused tourism. Through strategic partnerships and active conservation efforts, we safeguard our national parks and wildlife corridors for generations to come.

Conservation & Ecosystems

Wildlife Protection

Safeguarding Africa's largest mammal migration across 200,000 km^2 of protected wilderness, savannah corridors, and vital wetland ecosystems. This expansive conservation framework spans the Boma-Badingilo landscape and the Sudd wetlands, serving as the seasonal transit route for over six million white-eared kob, tiang, and Mongalla gazelle.

Vast Wilderness & Biodiversity

South Sudan hosts tiang, white-eared kob, Mongalla gazelle, elephants, lions, and hundreds of bird species. The Ministry works with global conservation partners to protect migration corridors and stop illegal poaching.
