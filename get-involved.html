PARTNERSHIPS & COMMUNITY

Get Involved in Conservation

Join hands with the Ministry, local communities, and global partners to protect South Sudan’s natural heritage and support sustainable eco-tourism.

Conservation Partnerships

Collaborate with park rangers, research institutions, and international environmental organizations to preserve crucial migratory wildlife corridors and ranger stations safeguarding the Nile migration.

Community Eco-Guides

Empower pastoral communities by supporting hospitality and tour guide training programs for youth, driving sustainable local economic development.

Volunteer & Fieldwork

Participate in accredited research, wildlife censuses, and environmental education projects studying the Sudd RAMSAR wetland ecosystem across national parks.
