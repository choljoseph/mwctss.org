SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT

Tourism Investment Opportunities

Partner with the Ministry of Wildlife Conservation and Tourism to build eco-lodges, wilderness camps, and sustainable tourism infrastructure across South Sudan.

Key Pillars

Priority Sector Opportunities

South Sudan offers untapped potential in eco-tourism, hospitality, and conservation infrastructure along key wildlife migration routes.

Eco-Lodges & Tented Camps

Concessions for high-end, low-impact luxury eco-lodges along the Boma-Badingilo wildlife corridor and Nimule National Park along the Nile.

River & Lake Transport

Development of passenger boat charters, Nile safari cruises, and sport fishing logistics connecting Juba to the Sudd wetlands.

Conservation & Anti-Poaching

Public-Private Partnerships (PPP) for park management, ranger training facilities, and satellite monitoring technology.

Framework

Investor Guarantees & Support

Duty-Free Equipment Imports

Exemption of customs duties on capital equipment and construction materials dedicated to approved tourism development projects.

Long-Term Land Concessions

Secure renewable land leases available up to 25–50 years for hospitality projects within designated tourism buffer zones.

One-Stop Ministry Liaison

Streamlined permit processing, environmental clearance, and licensing facilitated directly through the Directorate of Tourism.

Profit Repatriation

Legal framework supporting foreign direct investment, guarantees against expropriation, and full repatriation of capital and dividends.
