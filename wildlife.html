BIODIVERSITY & CONSERVATION SPECIES

Wildlife of South Sudan

Explore South Sudan's primary species groups, from vast migratory herds across the eastern savannahs to endemic wetland species in the Sudd.

Migratory Herbivores

Great Nile Migration Ecosystem
Keystone Species

White-Eared Kob

Millions of white-eared kob travel across the Boma-Badingilo landscape in Earth's largest land mammal migration cycle.

Habitat: Boma & Badingilo Plains
Migratory Antelope

Tiang (Topi)

Traveling Alongside white-eared kob, large herds of tiang move through seasonal grazing grounds in the eastern corridors.

Habitat: Seasonal Floodplains
Savannah Antelope

Mongalla Gazelle

Small, swift ungulates adapted to open grassland ecosystems across Central and Eastern Equatoria state reserves.

Habitat: Equatorial Savannah

Predators & Megafauna

Protected Parks & Corridors
Apex Carnivores

African Lions & Cheetahs

Key predator populations follow the migratory ungulate herds through Boma, Badingilo, and Southern National Park ecosystems.

Habitat: Grassland & Woodland
Protected Megafauna

African Savannah Elephant

Elephant corridors traverse Southern National Park and Nimule, where anti-poaching forces monitor seasonal movements.

Habitat: Park Reserves & River Basins
Rare Mammals

Giraffe & African Wild Dog

Nubian giraffe herds and packs of endangered African wild dogs maintain vital strongholds across undisturbed park sectors.

Habitat: Wooded Savannah

Wetland & Aquatic Specialists

Sudd Marshes & White Nile
Endemic Antelope

Nile Lechwe

An aquatic antelope species specialized for deep marshes, found primarily within the expansive papyrus swamps of the Sudd.

Habitat: Sudd Swamps & Floodplains
Aquatic Mammals

Hippopotamus & Nile Crocodile

Abundant throughout the White Nile waters and Nimule National Park river bends, forming key aquatic biomass.

Habitat: River Systems & Channels

Avian & Mountain Wildlife

Imatong Range & Wetlands
Iconic Avian

Shoebill Stork

The Sudd wetland forms one of the premier globally recognized nesting grounds for this prehistoric-looking bird species.

Habitat: Papyrus Marshes
Highland Species

Imatong Primates & Birds

Montane forests in Eastern Equatoria shelter unique primates, forest duikers, and endemic bird species adapted to higher elevations.

Habitat: Imatong Mountains Forest
