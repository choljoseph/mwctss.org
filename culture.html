LIVING TRADITIONS

Culture & Heritage Expeditions

Explore South Sudan's rich tapestry of pastoral traditions, ancient kingdoms, and vibrant ethnic heritage through official cultural itineraries.

Featured Expeditions

Cultural Tours

Immersion journeys into the heartlands of pastoral communities along the Nile and Eastern Equatoria.

Central Equatoria

Terekeka — Mundari Cattle Culture

Experience the iconic cattle camps of the Mundari people along the White Nile. Discover traditional pastoralism, body art, and ash-cleaning rituals preserving a centuries-old way of life.

Duration: 3-5 Days Explore Tour →
Eastern Equatoria

Kapoeta — Toposa Heritage Tour

Journey into Eastern Equatoria to meet the Toposa pastoralists. Experience traditional architecture, intricate scarification art, and traditional gold mining techniques.

Duration: 4-6 Days Explore Tour →
Jonglei State

Bor — Dinka Pastoral Traditions

Visit the expansive Sudd basin cattle camps of the Dinka. Learn about symbolic horn-shaping, cattle poetry, and seasonal movements in Africa's largest swamp system.

Duration: 3-4 Days Explore Tour →
Imatong Region

Torit — Lotuko Mountain Villages

Traverse the stone-walled mountain villages of the Lotuko people nestled around the Imatong Mountains. Experience rainmaking traditions and hilltop fortress architecture.

Duration: 3-5 Days Explore Tour →
Royal Lineages

Historical Kingdoms

Travel through the sovereign histories, royal courts, and sacred customs of South Sudan's indigenous monarchies.

Azande Kingdom

Located in Western Equatoria, the Azande kingdom is famed for its historic royal court traditions, intricate woodcraft, herbal medicine, and rich oral histories.

View Kingdom Tour →

Chollo (Shilluk) Kingdom

Centering around Fashoda along the Nile, this ancient monarchy features a divine king (Reth) lineage spanning centuries of political and spiritual governance.

View Kingdom Tour →

Anyuak Kingdom

Reaching across the rivers of Pochalla and the eastern borderlands, guided by noble royal emblems (Ucwini) and riverine agricultural traditions.

View Kingdom Tour →
