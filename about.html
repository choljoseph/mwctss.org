About Our Ministry

Preserving Heritage,
Fostering Discovery

Our Institution

Protecting South Sudan’s Natural Legacy

The Ministry of Wildlife Conservation and Tourism is tasked with protecting South Sudan’s extraordinary natural heritage, managing vast protected ecosystems, and promoting community-focused tourism initiatives.

From safeguarding Africa's largest terrestrial mammal migration across the Boma-Badingilo corridors to supporting biodiversity in the Sudd wetlands, our mission combines eco-preservation with economic growth.

Guiding Principles

Vision, Mission & Core Values

Our Vision

To establish South Sudan as a leading destination for eco-tourism, wildlife protection, and heritage preservation in East Africa.

Our Mission

To sustainably manage wildlife resources, protect vital habitats, and develop inclusive tourism frameworks that benefit local communities.

Core Values

Conservation stewardship, transparency, sustainable economic development, and cultural heritage promotion.
